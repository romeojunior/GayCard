# 🌈 Gay Card

A fun, customizable card generator for LGBTQ+ people, allies, and anyone who wants to make their own.

## 💳 About

The Gay Card started as a purely personal project.

I wanted to make myself a little **Gay Card** that I could carry around and show as a gag. I also wanted to make **Straight Ally cards** for my sisters and cousins, who love and support me as a gay person.

The project is not intended to be official, serious, or authoritative. It's just something I thought would be funny and fun to make—and eventually, I thought other people might enjoy making their own too.

The cards are intended as humor, self-expression, and a celebration of LGBTQ+ identity and allyship. They are **not intended to demean, insult, discriminate against, or hurt anyone**.

If you want a Gay Card, a Gold Star Gay card, a Platinum Star Gay card, a Straight Ally card, or something completely different, make one that's yours. 🌈

## ⭐ Star System

The card may include optional **Star** designations as part of the project's humor.

These are playful labels rather than official LGBTQ+ classifications.

### ⭐ Gold Star Gay

A humorous slang term traditionally used for a gay person who has never had sexual contact with someone of the opposite sex.

### 💎 Platinum Star Gay

A playful extension of the Gold Star joke.

For the purposes of this project, **Platinum Star Gay** refers to someone who was born via Caesarean section and therefore, according to the joke, never passed through the "opposite-sex exit." 😂

The definitions are intentionally silly and are not intended to determine anyone's LGBTQ+ identity, sexual orientation, or worth.

You can use whichever designation you find funny—or none at all.

## 🔒 Privacy

Privacy is an important part of the project.

The planned card generator is intended to generate cards **entirely within the user's browser**.

Names, photographs, and other information entered into the generator should remain on the user's device and should not need to be uploaded to a server.

No account should be required to create a card.

## 🎨 Make Your Own

The project is intended to be customizable.

If you want to make your own version, you can modify the design, change the colors, choose a different flag, add your own details, or create an entirely different card.

You don't have to make your card exactly like mine.

**Make it yours.**
